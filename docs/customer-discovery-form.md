# Zario business interview form

Use this as a Google Form or a 10-minute interview. Ask about the business's current work before showing Zario. One response per business. Do not collect WhatsApp tokens, passwords, or customer phone numbers.

## Introduction shown to respondents

We are learning how businesses handle WhatsApp inquiries and appointments. This takes about 7 minutes. Your answers help us decide what to build. This is research, not a sales commitment. Contact details are optional and only used if you ask for a follow-up.

## Questions

1. **Business category** (required, single choice): Salon/barber; clinic; dental practice; tutor/academy; repair service; restaurant; retail shop; real estate; consultant; other (write in).
2. **How many people handle customer messages?** (required, single choice): Just me; 2–3; 4–10; more than 10.
3. **How many WhatsApp customer inquiries arrive on a usual working day?** (required, single choice): 0–5; 6–20; 21–50; more than 50; unsure.
4. **How do you handle WhatsApp today?** (required, multiple choice): WhatsApp Business mobile app; personal WhatsApp; shared phone; desktop/web; another inbox tool; other.
5. **What usually goes wrong?** (required, choose up to two): Slow replies; missed messages; missed appointments; reminders done manually; staff cannot coordinate; customer details get lost; no major problem; other.
6. **In the past 30 days, roughly how many bookings or sales did you lose because of those problems?** (required, single choice): None; 1–2; 3–5; 6+; cannot estimate.
7. **What do you do after closing hours?** (required, single choice): Reply from my phone; staff replies; reply next day; automated replies; other.
8. **Would you need your current business WhatsApp number to stay usable in the WhatsApp Business mobile app?** (required, single choice): Yes, essential; preferred; no; unsure.
9. **Which one improvement would be worth paying for first?** (required, single choice): Faster shared replies; bookings and reminders; follow-up with existing customers; reports; none.
10. **What do you currently pay each month for messaging/booking software, excluding WhatsApp message charges?** (required, single choice): Rs 0; Rs 1–3,000; Rs 3,001–7,000; Rs 7,001+; prefer not to say.
11. **If your top problem were demonstrably solved, what monthly price would you consider?** (required, single choice): Would not pay; under Rs 3,000; Rs 3,000–5,000; Rs 5,001–10,000; above Rs 10,000. Ask *after* question 9; do not suggest Zario's listed price first.
12. **Would you try a two-week assisted pilot on your real business workflow?** (required, single choice): Yes; maybe; no. A pilot may require Meta account access and message charges; details would be agreed before setup.
13. **What would make you stop using a new tool after one week?** (optional, long answer).
14. **May we contact you about a pilot?** (required, single choice): Yes; no.
15. **Business name, city, and contact number** (optional, short answer; show only if question 14 is Yes). Do not ask for customer data.

## Interviewer notes (private, do not show respondents)

- Record interview date, niche, and whether the respondent is the decision maker.
- Write one concrete example of a recent lost inquiry or booking. If none, record that.
- After showing a short demo, ask: “Which step would you actually use tomorrow?” Record the exact answer.
- Record whether they agreed to a pilot, whether they agreed to a monthly price, and whether a payment was actually collected. Interest and payment are separate outcomes.

## Review after 10–15 interviews

Group responses by niche. Compare daily volume, stated pain, need for mobile WhatsApp, willingness to pilot, and actual paid conversion. Choose one or two niches with the strongest evidence for a real onboarding test. Do not average 10 unrelated niches into one score.
